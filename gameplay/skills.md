# Skills

Skills level up as you play. Every skill level gives you **stats**, and most skills also unlock **effects** that get stronger as you level.

### Commands

* `/skills` opens the skills menu
* `/skills top` shows the top players
* `/stats` shows your stats

### Stats

Each skill level adds stat points. This is what **one point** of each stat does. Points from different skills add up.

| Stat | One point |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Speed | Increases your attack speed by 1.5% |
| Crit Chance | Gives you a 1% chance to deal crits |
| Crit Damage | Crits deal 0.33% more damage (crits start at 50%) |
| Defense | Take 0.13% less damage |
| Ferocity | Gives a 0.5% chance to deal damage twice |
| Health | Gives +1 health |
| Speed | Makes you move 0.5% faster |
| Strength | Deal 0.17% more damage |
| Wisdom | Increases xp gain by 1.33% |

<summary><strong>Alchemy</strong></summary>

**How to get XP:** Brew potions

**Max level:** 50 (2,759,065 XP in total)

| XP | Blocks / items |
| --: | --- |
| 1 | Nether Wart, Redstone, Glowstone Dust, Fermented Spider Eye, Gunpowder |
| 2 | Spider Eye |
| 3 | Sugar |
| 4 | Pufferfish, Magma Cream, Ghast Tear |
| 6 | Rabbit Foot, Glistering Melon Slice, Golden Carrot, Blaze Powder |
| 12 | Dragon Breath, Phantom Membrane |
| 20 | Turtle Helmet |

#### Stats

* **Crit Chance**: +2 per level (+100 at level 50)
* **Wisdom**: +1 per level (+50 at level 50)

#### Effects

* **Potionmaster** (unlocks at level 1): Brewed potions last 4% longer. At level 50: Brewed potions last 200% longer.
* **Mystic Resilience** (unlocks at level 1): 0.5% chance to ignore negative potion effects. At level 50: 25% chance to ignore negative potion effects.
* **Efficient Brewing** (unlocks at level 10): Potions take 1.5% less time to brew. At level 50: Potions take 61.5% less time to brew.

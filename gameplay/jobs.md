# Jobs

Jobs pay you money and job XP for things you already do in survival, like mining, farming or building.

### Commands

* `/jobs` opens the jobs menu
* `/jobs join <job>` joins a job
* `/jobs leave <job>` leaves a job
* `/jobs info <job>` shows what a job pays
* `/jobs stats` shows your job levels

### How pay works

* How many jobs you can hold at once depends on your rank. See [Ranks](ranks.md).
* Every job goes up to **level 200**.
* Pay goes up by **0.5% per job level**, so a level 200 job pays about double the amounts below.
* Every extra job you hold lowers your pay by **5%** in all of your jobs. One job pays 100%, two jobs pay 95% each and three jobs pay 90% each.
* The amounts below are what a level 1 player with one job gets.

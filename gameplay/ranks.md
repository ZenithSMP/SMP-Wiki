# Ranks

There are three kinds of rank on Zenith:

* **Playtime ranks** you earn by playing. Open the menu with `/rankup`.
* **Donor ranks** you buy once from the [store](https://store.zenithmc.gg/categories/ranks). They are permanent and work on every Zenith server.
* **Plus**, a membership you can stack on top of any rank. See [Plus](#plus) below.

Every rank has its own icon next to your name in chat and in the tab list.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you have a playtime rank and a donor rank, you get the **higher** limit of the two for homes, jobs, auctions and player warps. They don't add together.
{% endhint %}

## Playtime ranks

Everyone starts as **Guest**. Open `/rankup` and click the next rank once you meet all of its requirements. Ranking up **costs** the money shown, and you get keys as a reward.

## Plus

Plus is a membership that works with any rank, including Guest. Buy it from the [store](https://store.zenithmc.gg/categories/plus):

| Option | Price |
| --- | --: |
| 30 days, renews every month | £4.99 |
| 90 days, one payment | £12.99 |
| 360 days, one payment | £49.99 |
| Plus Token | £5.49 |

Top ups add to the Plus time you already have. A **Plus Token** is an item you can keep, trade, or redeem whenever you like for 30 days of Plus or for gems.

### What Plus gives you

* **10 gems** every 30 days.
* **Plus icon** next to your name in chat. It changes colour the longer you have had Plus. Pick a colour or hide it with `/plusicon`.
* **Armour glints** (`/glint`): glowing colours and effects on netherite armour. More unlock the longer you have had Plus.
* **Custom prefix colour** with `/prefix`.
* **Chat:** Gold and Grey chat colours and colour codes in chat.
* **Nickname** with `/nick`, including colours and hex colours.
* **Glow colours:** Aqua, Green and Red (`/glow`).
* **Emoji menu** with `/emoji`.
* **Commands:** `/hat`, `/enderchest`, `/craft`, `/trash`, `/ptime` and `/kittycannon`.
* **7 homes, 8 jobs and 8 auctions.**
* **Coloured item names** in the anvil.
* **Invisible and glowing item frames.**
* **Pets:** Armour Stand and Shulker.
* **Particles:** Black Hole.
* **Lounge warp** (`/warp lounge`) and the **Plus tag**.
* **Creative:** WorldEdit.
